Certificate lifecycle at a glance

  1. Application Submitted
    Citizen applies online with supporting documents
  2. Document Verification
    Officer reviews uploaded documents
  3. Officer Review & Decision
    Approved or rejected with a reason
  4. Certificate Generated, Signed & QR-Issued
    Hashed, digitally signed, QR bound, PDF generated
  5. Publicly Verifiable
    Anyone can verify instantly via QR or certificate number
